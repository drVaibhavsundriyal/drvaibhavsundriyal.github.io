About

A clinician's view, built into the product

From oncology EMRs to one of India's largest hospital networks, my work sits where clinicians, product teams and engineers meet.

I trained as a physiotherapist, earned my MHA at IIHMR Delhi, and moved into health tech to fix the software I saw clinicians struggle with.

Since then I've owned outpatient and inpatient EMR modules, delivered go-lives at leading hospital groups, and turned clinicians' feedback into clear requirements that engineers can build.

Today I advise hospitals, providers and pharma companies on Salesforce CRM, custom solutions and agentic AI. I prototype quickly with AI tools and keep every solution grounded in how care is actually delivered.

Clinician-first requirements

Workflows mapped at the bedside and in the OPD, then written as user stories and specs.

Adoption over features

Training, workflow fixes and feedback loops, because a clinical product only works when clinicians use it.

AI that fits the consult

Documentation, summaries, orders and decision support that live inside the EMR, without adding another screen.

"In healthcare IT, workflow integration beats intelligence every time."Working principle
